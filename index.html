Short Bio

I am an Assistant Professor in the mathematics department at CUNY Baruch College. Previously, I was a postdoc in the Computer Science department at UCSD, affiliated with the EnCORE Institute, where I was very fortunate to be advised by Barna Saha. I completed my PhD in Computer Science at UCLA in 2023 under the sage guidance of Raghu Meka. Prior to that I received my MS in Computer Science from UCSC in 2018 where I was very lucky to work with C. Seshadhri. I completed a BA in Computer Science and BA in Mathematics from UCSC in 2016. In an ancient time, I attended Santa Rosa Junior College before transferring to UCSC.

Research Interests

I am broadly interested in theoretical computer science with a special focus on sublinear algorithms and computational learning theory.
